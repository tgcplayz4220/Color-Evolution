# 🎨 Color Evolution Game

**Start small. Survive the chaos. Evolve beyond your limits.**

Welcome to **Color Evolution Game**, a single-player survival game where color is power — and your next encounter could be your last!

You begin your journey as a tiny **Red Level 1 circle** in a vast, grey arena. Your mission? Survive, collect XP, level up, and evolve into increasingly powerful colors. But don't get too comfortable. The arena is filled with other circles, and not everything you encounter is something you can defeat.

## 🔴🟠🟡🟢🔵 Evolve Your Way to the Top!

In Color Evolution Game, progression is everything. Begin as Red, work your way through the levels, and eventually unlock entirely new color tiers. Each evolution brings you one step closer to becoming a force to be reckoned with.

Your journey currently takes you through four color tiers:

- 🔴 **Red** — Every journey has to start somewhere.
- 🟠 **Orange** — You're getting stronger, but there are still bigger threats out there.
- 🟡 **Yellow** — You've come a long way. Can you keep climbing?
- 🟢 **Green** — You've reached a higher tier of evolution!
- 🔵 **Blue** — You're getting better! Keep going!

Each color contains **nine levels**, giving you plenty of progression to work toward. Earn experience, cross level thresholds, and keep pushing forward!

## ⚔️ Survive or Be Eaten

The arena isn't empty. Other circles roam the world, competing for survival just like you.

Eat weaker opponents to gain experience and advance your evolution. But be careful — stronger circles can eat you, too! Every encounter matters, and one wrong move could end your run.

Here's the twist: **a higher color tier doesn't automatically make you invincible.** A powerful-looking opponent can still be defeated by something even stronger. Learn the hierarchy, recognize dangerous enemies, and know when it's time to escape.

Food is scattered throughout the arena, giving you another way to gain XP and grow stronger. Whether you're hunting opponents or collecting food, every bit of progress counts.

## 📈 More Than Just Evolution

Survival is only part of the challenge. Color Evolution Game also features a persistent progression system that rewards you beyond individual encounters.

- **Experience Points (XP):** Collect food and defeat opponents to work toward your next level.
- **Evolution Points:** Earn points when you level up through the evolution ranks.
- **Account Levels:** Build your long-term progression through bot kills.
- **XP Bonuses:** Boost your experience gains with available bonuses and account-level progression.
- **Persistent Progress:** Your account progression is saved locally in your browser, so it can carry over between sessions on the same browser.

Every run is another opportunity to improve, evolve, and push your progress further.

## 🕹️ Simple Controls, Serious Survival

Color Evolution Game is designed as a **PC browser game**, with straightforward controls that let you focus on what matters most: survival.

- **W / A / S / D** — Move around the arena.
- Collect food to gain XP.
- Hunt weaker circles to gain additional experience.
- Avoid stronger enemies that can eliminate you.
- Keep leveling up and work toward the next color tier.

The controls are simple. Staying alive is the real challenge.

## 🚀 The Beginning of Something Bigger

This is the **first release** of Color Evolution Game, and it's the foundation for a much bigger vision.

The current build establishes the core survival gameplay, color-based evolution, bots, XP progression, and persistent account levels. Future updates may expand the color system even further, with new tiers such as **Blue, Purple, Brown, and Black** planned for later development.

There is also room for a more complete title screen, additional account progression features, and new ways to customize or boost your journey.

For now, the focus is on building a working game, testing its mechanics, and laying the groundwork for future updates.
